About This Directory

Who Runs Cleanroom MEP Engineering

An engineering standard for a measurement-heavy market — published because the publisher's own firms kept meeting cleanroom buyers holding proposals that could not testify: classes named for whole buildings, cascades described in adjectives, utilities listed as a footnote, certification left as the owner's surprise. This property exists to publish the standard whole — the five controls, the two ledgers of what cleanroom engineering can and cannot establish, the packet any buyer can request — in the open, so a design can be weighed deliberately, against any firm, ours included.

The Operator

Cleanroom MEP Engineering is operated by Apex Grid Engineering PLLC, a veteran-owned engineering firm headquartered in Queen Creek, Arizona (Arizona firm registration held with the Arizona Board of Technical Registration). The operator's family of brands — Apex Grid Engineering, Torch Engineering, Engides, Seal Anvil, and Stamp Forge — is the evaluated set listed in this directory today.

Founder & CEO: Jeremy “Chico” Mills — serial entrepreneur · USAF veteran · owner of 10+ companies. Jeremy founded and runs the operator; he is not a licensed professional engineer, and this site never presents him as one. Engineering work behind the listed firms is performed and sealed by their licensed PE benches — the licensure facts are stated per state and checkable on the board lookups linked throughout this site.

What This Site Will Never Do

  • Publish a review, rating, or ranking it cannot document — the evaluations here are methodology assessments of evidence, never invented customer reviews
  • Award a star, a score, or a badge distilled from documents — evidence grades as documented or undocumented, never as 4.6 of anything
  • Claim a local office, project, or credential that does not exist — coverage means licensure in the state named, plus fast delivery
  • Publish a price, a count, or a turnaround figure for work nobody has seen — the numbers here are the drivers, never invented totals
  • Sell placement — order here is the publisher's disclosed choice, never an auction
  • Multiply pages without the published standard, the sourced local facts, and the disclosure applied to every one

Who Publishes This Directory

Published by Apex Grid Engineering PLLC. The firms evaluated and listed on this site today are the publisher's own family of brands — this is an affiliated directory, not a neutral referee. The methodology below is the standard any engineering firm can be measured against, and it is the standard we hold our own firms to in public.
